ClearExam

A simple place for courses, exam slots, bookings, and marks. Admins set things up. Examiners open slots. Students book a seat.

Sign in Create account

What this site does

Made for a small college team. One admin can add courses and exams. Staff and students each use their own login.

You can

  • Sign up or sign in with email and password.
  • As admin, add courses and exams from the home page.
  • Edit or remove a course when details change.
  • Attach a marking list (rubric) to an exam.
  • Sign out from the top menu when you finish.

Quick start

New people should sign up first. After sign in, an admin can open Courses, add a course, then open Exams to add an exam and a rubric.
